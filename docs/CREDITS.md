# Credits

## Music

The background music is composed live in the browser by `src/audio/` (no recordings). Its
instruments are samples from the **FluidR3 GM** soundfont by **Frank Wen**, released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) licence, as
converted by [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts):
electric piano 1, acoustic bass, vibraphone, nylon guitar and acoustic grand piano
(`public/music/samples/`, one MP3 per note). The drums are synthesised.

## Fonts

Anton and Space Mono, from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).
