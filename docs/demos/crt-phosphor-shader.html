Demo: WebGL post-processing that imitates a real phosphor tube. Not wired into the project.

This demo needs WebGL2, which this browser doesn't provide.

What the shader layer does, pass by pass:
1. Persistence: each frame's new beam trace is merged into a buffer that fades exponentially, like phosphor that keeps glowing after the beam passes. The fade drifts slightly warmer, as P31 phosphor does.
2. Beam physics: the beam's brightness depends on how long it lingers, so the fast vertical edges of a square wave draw faint, just like on a real scope.
3. Bloom and halation: two blur levels (tight glow plus a wide haze) scatter light through the "glass".
4. Tube: curved screen (barrel distortion), a tone curve that turns bright cores almost white-mint, a graticule on the glass that catches nearby glow, fine scanlines, vignette and a little film grain.
